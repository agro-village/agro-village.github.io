Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers for our global partners regarding logistics, quality standards, and operations.

Logistics & Shipping

Delivery times vary based on the destination and shipping method (air vs. sea freight). Generally, air freight for samples takes 2-4 days, while sea freight for large bales takes 14-25 days. We provide detailed tracking and realistic timelines during the quoting phase.

We primarily operate under FOB (Free On Board) and CIF (Cost, Insurance, and Freight). However, depending on the client relationship and destination, we can negotiate EXW or DDP terms. Please consult our sales team for specific requirements.

Quality & Orders

Our standard Minimum Order Quantity for sea freight is one 20ft container. For air freight, particularly for trial orders or premium samples, the MOQ is 500kg. Mixed pallets can be arranged under specific conditions.

Quality is maintained through strict sun-drying protocols that ensure low moisture content to prevent mold, followed by professional compression and packaging in tight bales that block moisture and external contaminants during long transit times.
