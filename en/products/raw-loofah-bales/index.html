Raw Loofah (Raw Material Bales)

Our raw loofah is supplied in its natural, unprocessed form, tightly compressed into export-grade bales as shown in the images. Each bale contains high-quality loofah fibers with natural variations in size, texture, and density—exactly as harvested from the farm. This format is ideal for factories, workshops, and brands that process or cut loofah according to their own product specifications. We offer full flexibility, providing samples upon request and tailoring bale selection to meet your required quality level, fiber length, or density.
